### PrimeNG Best Practices and Guidelines

- Do not use pTemplate, use #template
- Do not use pButton directive, use p-button component
- Do not use pInputText directive, use p-inputText component
- Do not use pInputTextarea directive, use p-inputTextarea component
- Do not use pDropdown directive and do not use p-dropdown component, use p-select, important!
- Do not use pCalendar directive, use p-date-picker component
- Do not use pTable directive, use p-table component
